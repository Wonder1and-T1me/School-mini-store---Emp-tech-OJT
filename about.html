Our story

Made for the little moments that add up.

Alice School Campus Shop began as a small neighborhood shop near campus. Students would stop by for a pen before class, a fresh notebook before exams, or a last-minute project supply. We saw how much those everyday moments mattered.

So we built a store around them: a welcoming place to find reliable school essentials without stretching your budget. Today, we're still here for the early-morning checklist, the big assignment, and everything in between.
